Which Auto Glass Service Do You Need in Clifton?

Start with what you see. A chip smaller than a quarter usually gets a repair. A long crack, or anything in the driver’s view, usually gets a new windshield, and on most late-model cars a new windshield also means a camera calibration.

Windshield Replacement in Clifton

A new windshield is the fix for long cracks, breaks in front of the driver, cracks that reach the edge of the glass, and glass so pitted from road grit that low sun turns it white. It restores the glass that helps hold up the roof and backs the passenger airbag.

If your crack is longer than a dollar bill or sits in your line of sight, you need one. Left alone, every cold night and every blast of the defroster pushes the crack further, and a crack that blocks your view can get you a ticket.

Windshield Chip Repair in Clifton

Chip repair injects clear resin into a small break, cures it with UV light and stops it from spreading. You keep your original windshield and the factory seal around it.

This is for chips smaller than a quarter and short single cracks. Call within a week or two. After a few freezing nights, a fixable chip can split into a crack that only a new windshield will solve.

ADAS Calibration in Clifton

ADAS calibration aims the camera mounted behind your windshield so lane-keeping, adaptive cruise and automatic braking read lane lines and traffic correctly.

Most cars from the last several years with a camera near the mirror need it after new glass. Skip it and you may get dashboard warnings, or a lane-keep system that nudges you toward the shoulder.
