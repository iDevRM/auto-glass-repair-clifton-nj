What Does Windshield Replacement Involve?

Windshield replacement starts with taking off the wipers, the plastic cowl at the base of the glass and the side moldings. The tech cuts through the old adhesive, lifts the windshield out and trims the old urethane down to a thin, even layer.

The question we hear most during this part is “How long until I can drive it?” We answer that at the end. Any bare metal gets primer, a new bead of urethane goes around the frame, and the new glass is lowered in with suction handles so it sits evenly. The trim and wipers go back on, and you get a drive-away time before the tech leaves.

When Do You Need a New Windshield?

  • A crack longer than a dollar bill, or two cracks that cross.
  • Any chip or crack right in front of the driver.
  • A crack that runs out to the edge of the glass.
  • Glass sandblasted by years of winter grit, so oncoming headlights smear at night.
  • A leak or wind noise left behind by an earlier install.

Why Do Windshields Crack Around Clifton?

Most windshields here start with a stone. Plows spread salt and grit from December into March, and the traffic on Route 3, Route 46 and the Garden State Parkway throws it right back at the car behind.

Temperature swings finish the job. A chip that sits through a week of nights in the 20s, then gets hit by the defroster on a frosty morning, can stretch a foot across the glass before you reach the Allwood Park and Ride. Potholes in late winter flex the body and stress any chip near the edge.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I drive after a windshield replacement?

After a windshield replacement, most urethanes let you drive within half an hour to a few hours. Cold, damp days slow the cure, so the tech gives you a drive-away time for that day’s weather. Skip the car wash for a day or two.

Can you replace a windshield in the rain?

Windshield replacement needs dry glass and a dry frame for the urethane to bond. Light drizzle under a garage or carport is fine. In a real downpour or snowstorm we’ll move the appointment.

Does a new windshield affect my car’s cameras?

Yes. A new windshield moves the forward camera very slightly, so most cars with lane-keeping or automatic braking need calibration afterward. We confirm this for your vehicle before ordering the glass.

Will my insurance cover windshield replacement in New Jersey?

Windshield replacement in New Jersey is normally covered under comprehensive coverage, minus your deductible. We can help you open the claim with your insurer when you call.
