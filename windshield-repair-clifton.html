Windshield chip repair in Clifton fills small rock chips with clear resin so they stop spreading and get much harder to see. It takes about 30 minutes in your driveway, and you keep your factory windshield and its original seal.

What Does Windshield Chip Repair Involve?

A chip that showed up on Monday is easier to fix than one that has sat through two weeks of slush. The tech cleans the chip and pulls out loose glass, then mounts a small bridge tool over the break. The tool draws air out of the chip and pushes resin deep into every leg of the break.

A UV lamp hardens the resin, the surface is scraped flat and polished, and that’s it. The chip won’t vanish completely, but it usually fades to a faint mark and, more important, it stops growing.

When Do You Need Chip Repair?

  • A bullseye, star or combination chip smaller than a quarter.
  • A single crack shorter than about six inches.
  • Damage that isn’t right in the driver’s line of sight.
  • A chip that’s still clean, ideally less than a couple of weeks old.

Why Do Small Chips Turn Into Cracks?

A chip is a weak point in the outer layer of glass. When the glass expands and shrinks, stress gathers at that spot until it gives and a crack runs outward.

Winter here is the usual trigger. A frosty car with the defroster cranked, a slammed door, or one more pothole on Main Avenue can send a chip running. Dirt and water that collect inside the chip also make the resin bond weaker, which is one more reason not to wait.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does windshield chip repair take?

Windshield chip repair takes about 30 minutes for one chip, and you can drive right after. A few extra chips on the same windshield add a little time each.

Will the chip disappear after repair?

A repaired chip usually fades to a faint mark rather than disappearing. The main goal is structural: the resin stops the chip from spreading into a crack.

Can a chip be repaired in cold weather?

Chip repair works in cold weather as long as the glass is dry and the tech warms the area gently first. Extreme cold or ice on the glass can mean rescheduling for a milder day.
